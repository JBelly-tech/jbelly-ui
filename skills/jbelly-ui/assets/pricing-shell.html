Asked before signing

Can we change plan in the middle of a billing period?

An upgrade takes effect the moment you confirm it, and we charge only the difference for the days left in the period. A downgrade takes effect on the next renewal date, so you keep what you already paid for.

What counts as an order?

One order created in a connected store, whatever number of items it holds. Cancellations, refunds and orders made in the sandbox are not counted.

What happens if we go past the monthly order limit?

Orders keep syncing and nothing is blocked. We email the account owner in the same week, and if you are over the limit two months in a row we ask you to move up a plan.

Do extra seats cost more?

Starter and Growth include the seats listed in the table; each seat after that is $6 a month. Scale has no seat limit, so adding people changes nothing on the invoice.

How do we cancel, and what happens to our data?

Cancel from Settings, Billing, with no phone call and no retention script. Exports stay available for 30 days after the last billing day, then the workspace is deleted.
